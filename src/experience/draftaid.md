- §Brand research and development
- **Own the brand system and the brand engine** so messaging stays consistent across teams and channels, and Draftaid reads as a trusted, competent engineering brand. Vision and messaging drive type, color, and the visual system. Claude skills and design tools carry that system into every touchpoint.
- **Treat branding as ongoing R&D, not a one-time rollout.** When a new application shows where the system breaks, the guidelines update before that inconsistency reaches prospects or current customers.
- **Aimed the system at how the team actually works.** Most people make **3+ customer-facing assets a week**, **33% make 6+**, and about **55% use AI tools**, often with poor results when design skill is missing. The team already knows why consistency matters. Shared skills and templates are how it happens.
- §Marketing I own
- **Own marketing across the website, social, newsletter, PR, and tradeshows.** Three goals: more qualified inbound with sales, stronger customer relationships with customer success, and a trusted engineering brand.
- **Rebuilding the marketing website in Webflow**, applying the brand so the site stays consistent and ready to convert.
- §Supporting other teams
- **Sales:** revamping the deck, slide template, and outbound one-pagers, rolling new messaging through every touchpoint, and designing tradeshow logistics, the booth, flyers, banners, and business cards so inbound matches the brand.
- **Customer success:** redesigning the kickoff deck, building slide templates both teams use, and designing case studies and swag.
- **Engineering and product:** designed a product icon system from the brand language, and bringing the brand into the client app with Michael, matched to his component system.
